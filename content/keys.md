PGP lets you encrypt messages and files and verify digital signatures. Encrypting a message with my public key lets me decrypt it with my private key. Verifying a signature helps you check that a message or file was signed by me and has not changed.

Feel free to use it whenever you want to or to verify something I have signed. To get started, [set up OpenPGP in Thunderbird](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/openpgp-thunderbird-howto-and-faq), import my public key below, and compare its full fingerprint with me over another trusted channel. Then enable encryption when composing your message.

You can also find my key on [keys.openpgp.org](https://keys.openpgp.org/search?q=oskar%40onderka.dev).
